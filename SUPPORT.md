# Support and privacy

Before reporting a problem, please include:

- Your MediaCheck version
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Whether the problem can be repeated
- The approximate library size and storage type, without posting private paths

Select **Send feedback** in MediaCheck or open the [GitHub report form](../../issues/new/choose), then choose **Bug report**. Nothing is uploaded or submitted automatically; you decide exactly what to share.

## Creating a support bundle

1. Open MediaCheck.
2. Select **Diagnostics** near the top-right corner.
3. Review the Support bundle preview.
4. Select **Export support bundle**.
5. Open the resulting ZIP and inspect its text files before attaching it to an issue.

The bundle contains a diagnostic summary, privacy explanation, and recent application log when available. It excludes video and audio files, preview images, scan-cache contents, scan-history contents, and configured library paths. Home and configured paths in the optional log are replaced with `%REDACTED_PATH%`.

Never upload media merely to report a MediaCheck bug. Filenames and screenshots can also be private, so obscure them when necessary.
